# Alpian

> Alpian is Switzerland's first digital premium bank, combining everyday banking with personal wealth management in one app. FINMA-licensed, majority-owned by Fideuram - Intesa Sanpaolo Private Banking. Positioned between retail banking and traditional private banking: private banking services accessible from CHF 2,000. Tagline: Banking Upgraded.

Alpian serves clients in four languages (English, German, French, Italian) across Switzerland. Core differentiators: multi-currency accounts (CHF, EUR, USD, GBP) with a 0.20% FX markup and no hidden fees, professionally managed investing from CHF 2,000 minimum, access to real human wealth advisors included, and a Visa Platinum metal debit card. Alpian is a fully licensed Swiss bank supervised by FINMA; client deposits are protected by the Swiss depositor protection scheme (esisuisse) up to CHF 100,000.

Alpian is not a robo-advisor and not a traditional private bank. It is a digital premium bank: digital-first everyday banking plus discretionary and advisory wealth management with human advisors, for people who want more than retail banking without the entry barriers of private banking.
